<p class="text-black text-base leading-normal md:text-xl lg:text-2xl  xl:text-2xl font-normal pb-10 w-full max-w-[46rem] w-full company-blurb">
<a class='underline hover:text-pink-500' href='https://simple.org/'>Simple</a> is a contextual digital platform supported by <a class='underline hover:text-pink-500' href='https://resolvetosavelives.org/'>Resolve to Save Lives (RTSL),</a> which was formerly incubated by <a class='underline hover:text-pink-500' href='https://www.vitalstrategies.org/'>Vital Strategies</a> and works to improve global cardiovascular health.
</p>

<div class="border-t border-b border-gray-200 py-10">
<p class="text-pink-500 text-base leading-normal md:text-xl lg:text-xl md:leading-snug font-light">
Simple is an offline-first Android app and web dashboard that enables policy makers to monitor and improve hypertension and diabetes outcomes.
</p>
</div>

<div class="md:pt-0 md:pb-0 py-10">
<p class="md:pt-12 text-black text-base leading-normal md:text-xl lg:text-xl md:leading-snug font-light tpb-4 md:pb-7">
nilenso has worked with Simple since its inception in 2018, from building the first prototype, to launching the pilot solution, iteratively developing features, managing scale, as well as playing a pivotal role in Simple's expansion beyond India to other countries.
</p>

<p class="text-black text-base leading-normal md:text-xl lg:text-xl md:leading-snug font-light pb-4 md:pb-7">
We designed the fully offline-first sync APIs that allow for the app to be used in rural areas with limited internet connectivity.
</p>

<p class="text-black text-base leading-normal md:text-xl lg:text-xl md:leading-snug font-light pb-4 md:pb-7">
In addition, we built the dashboards that support both population-level administration by policy experts as well as more granular monitoring by health officials.
</p>

<p class="text-black text-base leading-normal md:text-xl lg:text-xl md:leading-snug font-light pb-4 md:pb-7">
The dashboards were engineered for performance as well as accuracy using ETL pipelines as well as Russian doll caching.
</p>
</div>
